# Caldova simulator design

The learner simulator is a restrained Operate surface for a bright classroom or workstation environment.

- Use a cool white work surface, navy text, neutral dividers, and one azure action color.
- Keep the task in one continuous split-pane runbook: setup on the left, state and actions on the right.
- Prefer standard inputs, radios, selects, links, progress bars, and buttons.
- Status is always expressed with text in addition to color.
- Errors remain inline and name both the failure and the next action.
- Long-running operations expose their current real phase, progress, cancellation, and timing expectations.
- Avoid card grids, decorative motion, gradients, glass effects, and invented controls.
- At narrow widths, stack setup above the runbook and preserve full-width controls.
